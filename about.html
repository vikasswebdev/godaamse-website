About GodaamSe

We are on a mission to empower every shopkeeper with the digital tools and direct sourcing they need to thrive in the modern era.

The Shopkeeper's Partner

GodaamSe is the ultimate online store built exclusively for shopkeepers. We understand the challenges of running a Kirana store—from visiting wholesale markets to managing credits and inventory.

Our platform bridges the gap between top brands and local retailers. By providing direct sourcing, we ensure shopkeepers get better margins and authentic products, delivered straight to their shops.

Beyond sourcing, we provide a complete digital ecosystem: inventory management, sales tracking, digital Khata, and credit solutions. We're not just a platform; we're a growth partner for your business.

Our Mission

To digitize and empower the millions of shopkeepers who form the backbone of our economy. We believe in providing them with the same technological advantages and sourcing power that large retail chains enjoy.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted digital platform for shopkeepers globally, enabling a seamless transition to digital commerce while increasing profitability and ease of doing business.
